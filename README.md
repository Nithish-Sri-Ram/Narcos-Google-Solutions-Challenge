# Narcos - AI-Driven Drug Discovery Platform  
**Accelerating Drug Discovery through AI-Powered Prediction and Collaborative Research**

Narcos is an open-source, AI-powered platform designed to accelerate early-stage drug discovery. Instead of generating entirely new molecules, Narcos leverages researcher-provided SMILES strings to predict key properties of drug candidates and facilitate molecular exploration. It integrates predictive modeling, real-time collaboration, and natural language interaction to streamline the drug development process.

---

## 🧠 Our Solution

Our platform focuses on optimizing the drug discovery process by:
- **Predicting molecular behavior** using ADMET and binding affinity models.
- **Enabling structured molecular exploration** from SMILES input combinations.
- **Building a collaborative open-source community** inspired by GitHub and Reddit.
- **Integrating LLMs** for intelligent interaction with models and research findings.

## 🌟 Opportunities and USP of Our Proposed Solution

- **Faster Time-to-Discovery**: Rapidly eliminate unviable compounds early in the pipeline.
- **Community-Driven Innovation**: Unlock faster iteration through open collaboration.
- **Research Democratization**: Lower entry barriers with open access tools and data.
- **Unique Value**: Focus on prediction, not generation. Open-source, collaborative, and LLM-enhanced.
